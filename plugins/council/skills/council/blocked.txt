# Paths that must never go to outside reviewers (OpenAI, Google), on top of the built-in checks for secrets,
# keys, certificates and dependency folders.
#
# One pattern per line: a case-insensitive regular expression tested against each file's path, both relative to
# the repo and absolute. Lines starting with # are comments. Add your client folders, confidential projects and
# anything under regulated data handling.

# Folders and files whose names say they are not for sharing.
(^|[\\/])(confidential|internal[-_]only|do[-_]not[-_]share|nda)([\\/._-]|$)
# Data exports, dumps and backups usually hold real people's data.
\.(sql|dump|bak|sqlite|db)$
(^|[\\/])(exports?|dumps?|backups?)([\\/]|$)
