# Your design eye

The one global layer. Every project gets its own design system, but each one is drafted inside these
rules. Fill this file with your own taste: things you said or chose, each with a date, so later
sessions know where a rule came from. When a project's brief clearly calls for something else (a
client's real brand, a game), the brief wins. Say which rule you are breaking and why.

Until you fill it in, the skill works from the brief and the psychology research alone, and asks a
little more at the draft step.

## What you reach for

Write one bullet per preference, with the reason and a date. Some examples of the kind of rule that
helps, to replace with your own:

- **Ground and ink.** For example: white ground, near-black ink, neutral greys, one vivid accent. Name
  your default type pair.
- **What cohesion means to you.** For example: one card treatment repeated exactly, one radius family,
  flat solid buttons, the accent on two elements per screen at most.
- **Where energy comes from.** For example: one hot accent spent in a few large fields, plus motion
  that means something, never gradients or glow.
- **Type and labels.** For example: a real semantic type scale, headings with weight, body text in
  real ink, mono labels in uppercase with wide tracking.
- **Products you admire, and what exactly you take from each.**
- **Real assets over invented ones.** Real photos, the client's real logo, real screenshots.

## What reads as slop to you

List what you reject on sight, with an example of where you saw it. Common entries: glow orbs and
grain, every section a different treatment, clever metaphors that read as AI tropes, emoji in the UI,
pillowy radii everywhere, dev chrome in the product (version pills, port numbers), decorative
motion.

## Copy

How you want product copy to sound: length, tone, punctuation, what you never write.

## How you work with design

- Your eye comes in at the concept pick. Show distinct options, let you choose and mix, then build
  only from what you chose.
- Other skills are advisors. Their rules guard against slop, and your picks and this file outrank
  them.
- Before calling anything done, look at it rendered at the sizes it ships at.
